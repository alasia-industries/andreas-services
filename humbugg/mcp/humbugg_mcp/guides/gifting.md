# Wish lists and giving on Humbugg

**Your own list.** In each group you have wishes (`my_wishlist`, `add_wish`,
`update_wish`, `delete_wish`, `reorder_wishes`, or the `edit_wishlist` page) and two
free-text notes (`set_wishlist_notes`): general wishes such as sizes and tastes, and
"please don't give" — allergies, things you already have. Your giver sees all of it.

**Who you drew.** After the draw, `my_assignment` (or the `show_assignment` page) shows the
person you are buying for, their list and notes, and their address when the group posts
gifts. Mark a wish as planned or bought with `claim_wish` so you don't buy it twice —
only you see your claims. Track your progress with `set_gift_stage` (choosing →
purchased → sent); the organizer sees only totals.

**Anonymous questions.** `questions` reads both threads. `ask_my_recipient` asks the person
you drew something (they see "Your Secret Santa"); `answer_my_secret_santa` replies to
whoever drew you. Both notify the other person, so send only what the member approved.

**Your Secret Santa is secret.** Nothing in Humbugg says who drew you, and nothing should
guess. When your gift arrives, `mark_gift_received` lets your giver know.
